Configure the "Employee Advance" product with a Current Asset account and
enable Allow Reconciliation before using this module.

**Create an Employee Advance**

1. Go to Expenses \> My Expenses \> My Advances and create an advance.
2. Enter the advance amount. Optionally, select a Clearing Product to prefill
   the product on the clearing expense.
3. Submit, approve, and post the advance.
4. Register the payment to the employee.
5. The paid advance shows its Remaining to Clear amount.

**Clear Advance**

You can create a clearing in two ways:

1. From an advance:

   - Open a paid advance with a Remaining to Clear amount.
   - Click Clear Advance. A new expense opens with the employee and advance
     prefilled. The Clearing Product is also prefilled when configured.
   - Complete, submit, and approve the expense.

2. From the clearing menu:

   - Go to Expenses \> My Expenses \> My Clearings and create an expense.
   - Select the advance in Clear Advance.
   - Complete, submit, and approve the expense.

Post Journal Entries and select a Miscellaneous journal in the Post Expenses
wizard. Normal expenses and advance clearings must be posted separately.

Note:

- If the clearing amount is less than or equal to the advance, the clearing is
  set to Paid after posting.
- If the clearing amount exceeds the advance, the clearing shows Amount to Pay.
  Click Pay and register the difference to set the clearing to Paid.

**Return Advance**

1. Open a paid advance with a Remaining to Clear amount.
2. Click Return Advance to open the Register Payment wizard.
3. Enter an amount that does not exceed Remaining to Clear and click Create
   Payment.
4. The inbound payment is linked to the advance and is available from the
   Return smart button.
